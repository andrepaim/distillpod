DistillPod 0.25.0-alpha.1 adds a sleep timer, playback effects and per-show speeds, a home-screen widget, and search across your local library. It also improves storage cleanup, queue controls, saved moments and sync recovery.

- **Sleep timer.** Pause after 5, 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes of playing time, or at the end of the current episode. The last 15 seconds fade out; a manual pause freezes the countdown. Extend it by five minutes or cancel from Now Playing. The service owns the timer, so navigating away does not cancel it; it ends if Android stops the service and is not restored after process death.
- **Playback speed and effects.** Adjust speed in 0.1× steps between 0.5× and 3×, retain the existing presets, or remember a default for each show. Skip silence and a moderate volume boost are optional and off by default. An unavailable boost leaves ordinary playback working and shows an explanation. These preferences are stored on this device and are excluded from backup and sync.
- **Home-screen widget.** See the current show and episode, play/pause, open Now Playing, or save the last 60 seconds as a moment. Actions use the live media session; a stale widget refreshes after a tap. Episode and show titles are visible on the home screen.
- **Search your library.** The Search tab finds followed shows, episodes, prepared transcript passages and saved moments locally. Tap a passage to open its transcript. Public podcast-directory search remains an explicit action. Matches are bounded; the search is not an exhaustive export of the library.
- **Clear local audio after review.** Settings → Storage previews completed and unfinished downloads and managed audio cache, then asks for confirmation. Only the reviewed selection is eligible. Playing, queued, processing and automatically preparing audio is protected; new or changed copies are kept. Transcripts, saved insights, models and listening progress remain. Partial results are reported, and cancelling cannot restore files already deleted. Bulk transcript cleanup and the new configurable retention rules remain unfinished.
- **Clearer pages and queue controls.** Saved has separate Moments and Posts tabs, compact cards and a simpler moment page. Moment deletion asks for confirmation. Queue rows expose move/remove actions and TalkBack actions. Library resume cards agree with the player, dates are consistent, episode actions are easier to reach, and feed errors identify the affected followed shows with recovery actions. Settings has compact accessible group headings and a regular About row.
- **Permission recovery.** The first explicit download or transcript preparation can explain and request notification access on Android 13+. Denial does not block work or trigger repeated prompts; Settings remains available. Voice microphone denial offers system app settings when needed. Android 13+ Back handling is enabled.
- **Playback and sync reliability.** Measured episode durations survive refreshes of unchanged played media. Playback readiness is released even when startup restoration fails. Continuous playback checkpoints occur about every ten seconds, with immediate saves at pause, seek and other boundaries. Sync triggers coalesce, Sync now can replace retry-backoff work, and refused changes are set aside with Retry/Discard controls so later changes proceed. Restored records become eligible for normal sync, query-bearing episode locators are handled without syncing private query parameters, and unchanged transcript artifacts avoid repeated rebuilding.
- **Privacy and maintenance.** Android Auto artwork is handed to accepted browsers through individual read grants. Unchanged refresh configuration retains its schedule, cancelled refresh-all stops promptly, and storage/download errors provide more useful recovery guidance. Several validation fixtures were corrected without weakening their assertions.

The intended signed update uses version code **48**, preserving the application ID and signing certificate. Database schema **12** and portable backup schema **7** are unchanged. Voice preview remains enabled for distribution; content uploads and history compaction remain disabled. Protocol-4 history recovery support is included, but this release neither upgrades accounts for compaction nor deletes cloud history. Enabling compaction requires a separately authorized rules deployment and a later explicitly enabled build. No backend deployment is required for this update.

**Known limits:** abrupt process death can lose roughly ten seconds of continuous playback progress. File removal and database updates are not one atomic operation, so cleanup reports partial outcomes. Sync backoff replacement retains a narrow start race; offline and Android background restrictions still apply. A failed sync scheduling chain may need another trigger or periodic recovery. Audio effects vary by phone. Android Auto artwork has emulator grant coverage rather than a real car-host/DHU check. No measured battery-life improvement or live-account validation is claimed.

**Validation:** the default and voice-enabled configurations passed unit tests, lint and APK assembly on the combined candidate, together with 160 tooling tests. Eight real-WorkManager scheduling scenarios passed on an isolated API35 emulator and a Galaxy S22 Ultra running Android16: foreground/background bursts, one follow-up to active work, pause, actual retry backoff, Sync now replacement, and synthetic-state recovery after process stop/reopen. The offline phone probe used a separate app and synthetic worker outcomes; the owner's DistillPod installation was unchanged. These checks establish scheduling behavior, not live cloud delivery or battery life. Feature-specific S22 evidence also covers cleanup, checkpoints, queue, timer, effects, widget and notification permissions at their recorded implementation revisions. No owner-app upgrade or live-account test is claimed. Predictive Back gesture rendering and real Android Auto artwork host/DHU behavior remain unverified. Publication separately validates the tagged source, release build and signed APK.
