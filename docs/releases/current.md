DistillPod 0.23.0-alpha.1 makes it easier to organize Shows and manage the playback queue, and improves recovery when Android delays transcription.

- **Find and organize shows.** Filter audio and video, sort by title, last played or latest episode, and pin shows to the top of the current results. Cards show format, latest known episode date and unfinished count. Filters, sorting and pins are stored on each installation.
- **Manage the whole queue.** Reorder items in playback order, including the current episode. Clear queue and stop playback asks for confirmation and preserves downloads and listening progress. The app protects a queue order you explicitly choose during sync from older pending playback writes. Later edits still take precedence.
- **Recover transcription.** When Android rejects foreground startup, DistillPod retries with backoff and retains saved chunks. After five rejections, it pauses the job and offers Resume. Late state updates also respect deleted episodes and newer transcript revisions.
- **Get clearer feedback.** Voice questions explain missing or stale transcript context before interrupting playback. Download removal explains when audio is protected by playback, the queue, transcription or a download in progress. TalkBack announces the diagnostics, usage-statistics and rewind controls as labeled switches.

Install the signed APK as an update over your existing DistillPod installation. Version code **41** retains the application ID and signing identity. The app upgrades the local database from schema 11 to 12 to record when new audio copies and transcript revisions are created. Existing content keeps an unknown age, and automatic cleanup remains disabled. Portable backups stay at schema 7 and exclude these local timestamps. Firebase protocol, rules and sync scope stay at the previous release's versions. The APK retains the voice preview and its setup, credit and transcript requirements. The source archive, component manifest and notices accompany the APK.

Queue writes remain asynchronous and can be lost if Android kills the process before they finish. WorkManager decides when a transcription retry runs. Feature validation includes emulator checks and isolated Galaxy S22 runs for queue controls, transcription recovery and lifetime, voice readiness, protected deletion, settings switches and the schema migration. The migration checks used synthetic data. Coverage of a signed upgrade on the owner's installation and live cross-device delivery is pending. The ChatGPT integration is still in development.
