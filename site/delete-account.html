Delete your DistillPod data

DistillPod has no account of its own and keeps your library on your phone. This page explains how to delete each kind of data the app can hold. Developer: Andre Paim Lemos.

Data on your phone

Uninstall DistillPod, or open Android Settings → Apps → DistillPod → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes your library, downloads, transcripts, saved moments, AI results and stored sign-in tokens.

ChatGPT connection

Open Settings → ChatGPT → Disconnect account in DistillPod to delete the stored sign-in tokens. Your conversations and data at OpenAI are managed in your OpenAI account, not by DistillPod.

Library sync account and synced data

If you turned on library sync, a sign-in account and your synced library records are stored in Google Firebase. To delete them:

  1. Send an email to andrepaim@gmail.com from the Google account you used for sync, with the subject "Delete my DistillPod sync data".
  2. We delete the sign-in account and every synced record for it (subscriptions, listening progress, saved moments, queue and preferences) within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Signing out in Settings → Sync stops sync but does not delete data already stored.

To delete part of the data without deleting the account, unfollow a show or delete a saved moment in the app; the change is synced.

Diagnostics

Turn off Settings → Diagnostics to stop reports and delete those not yet sent. Reports already delivered contain no account identity and expire under Sentry's retention period.
