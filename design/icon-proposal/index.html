The thinking

Two ideas.
One small mark.

The drop represents the essence of a conversation. Its open center is a rounded play symbol. Together, they connect DistillPod’s name to what you actually do: listen and keep the worthwhile parts.

The exploration

Three routes.
A clear favorite.

I explored listening, sound, and saved ideas. The drop offers the strongest connection between the product name and the listening experience.

The recommendation
01 / ESSENCE

The listening drop

The name and the purpose in one silhouette. Distinctive enough to become a recognisable signature.

Best connection to DistillPod
02 / SOUND

The signal D

A geometric D built from a signal and a receiver. Clear and compact, though it could also belong to an audio hardware brand.

Strong monogram; less expressive
03 / MEMORY

The audio bookmark

A saved moment, with sound inside. A useful idea for the Saved area, but it makes the whole app feel like a note-taking tool.

Useful secondary visual language

The handoff

Small mark.
Thought-through system.

The proposal includes editable vector masters, raster previews, and separate Android foreground, background, and monochrome layers.
